## Release use

- The catalog tells the runtime which implemented visual belongs to each lesson and gives teachers the intended teaching use.
- A visual reaches release sign-off only after the teacher verifies the mechanism and terminology, the visual team verifies every important state, accessibility verifies keyboard/screen-reader/reduced-motion behavior, and QA verifies deterministic reset and edge cases.
- The `usage`, `learningFocus` and `teacherNote` fields are bilingual and are derived from the matching lesson file; update the lesson and catalog together when the instructional contract changes.
